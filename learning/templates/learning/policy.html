Privacy information

CBE STEM ACADEMY provides learning resources, virtual practicals and private saved work. The academy is responsible for account administration. {% if support_email %}Contact: {{ support_email }}.{% else %}Public privacy contact is being configured; contact academy staff through Help before submitting sensitive information.{% endif %}

Information and purposes

We use usernames, password hashes, recovery email, age band, consent records, selected grade, class memberships, saved work, submitted copies and feedback to operate the learning service. We do not request full birth dates or identity documents through these forms. Teachers can access work submitted to their assigned classes. Authorised administrators manage accounts, support and security.

Devices and processing

Browser storage keeps work and offline recovery copies separately for each account. Old device-only work is imported only when explicitly selected. Handwriting is sent to this website’s recognition server. Optional browser voice recognition may use your browser provider’s speech service after you start the microphone. Ask AI has been removed. We do not use advertising or behavioural analytics.

Storage and service providers

Public learning uploads have public addresses. Private learner attachments use authenticated downloads. The deployment is designed to use an OVHcloud server, Resend transactional email and encrypted Cloudflare R2 backups. Hosting, email or backups may process information outside Kenya. The operator must document actual locations, contracts and transfer safeguards before public launch; this page does not claim regulatory approval. Email links are used for verification and recovery; open/click tracking must be disabled.

Retention and rights

Abandoned signup requests are removed after seven days. Operational logs are kept no longer than 30 days. Approved deletion requests are completed within 30 days; older encrypted backups expire through their 30-day retention schedule. Contact support to request access, correction, consent review or deletion. Account removal includes private work and assessment copies; the academy reviews legal retention obligations and explains any necessary exception before finalising a request. Browser exports and copies you download are under your control.

{% elif kind == 'terms' %}

Terms of use

Use CBE STEM ACADEMY for respectful learning. Keep login details private, upload only materials you may lawfully use, and do not submit another learner’s work as your own. A learner account never grants teacher or administrator privileges.

Virtual experiments are teaching models and do not replace supervised real laboratory practice. Follow laboratory safety instructions from a qualified teacher. Automatically checked answers are practice feedback; teacher assessment is shown separately. Lesson completion is self-reported.

Resources may include third-party material with its own licence or attribution. Retain those conditions when reusing it. Service interruptions may occur; keep downloadable work backups. Contact support to report inaccurate content, access problems or misuse.

{% elif kind == 'guardians' %}

Guardian information

Under-18 learners start with a parent’s or authorised guardian’s recovery email. Before a new learner supplies a username or password, the guardian receives an expiring link, reads these policies and explicitly approves account creation. Existing accounts complete the same verification before new online saving.

Possession of an email address is not proof of legal guardianship. Academy staff review disputes and may verify authority through an established school or guardian contact. Do not upload identity documents through support. Guardians may ask about saved work, review consent or request deletion through support.

The Kenyan children’s-data requirements, transfer safeguards and any applicable registration or impact assessment must be reviewed by the operator before public collection. This technical approval flow is not a certification of compliance.

{% else %}

Account deletion information

Signed-in learners can request deletion from My Learning using their password. Guardians can contact support. Staff verify authority, review any retention obligation, and complete approved requests within 30 days. Online saving pauses after the request. Export work beforehand; deleted work cannot be promised recoverable.

Encrypted backups follow a separate 30-day expiry schedule. Copies you downloaded or retained in another browser require removal on that device. Staff accounts need an administrator to transfer responsibilities first.
