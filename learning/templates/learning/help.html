Help and support

Accounts and guardians

Choose Create account. Adults verify their own email. Learners under 18 start with a guardian’s email. The guardian reviews the information and explicitly approves before the learner chooses a username and password.

Approval links expire in 24 hours. Use Resend approval if needed. For forgotten passwords, enter your username and verified recovery email. If you cannot use email, ask academy staff for identity-checked recovery.

Whiteboard and graphs

Use the labelled drawing, text and equation tools. Graph supports linear, quadratic and cubic functions and tables of values. Keyboard-accessible inputs provide an alternative to drawing. Review handwriting suggestions before accepting them. Undo restores edits.

File → Download editable board keeps a portable backup, including supported document attachments. An image export does not preserve editable elements.

Practical activities

Read the objective, make your prediction, collect apparatus and follow the learner procedure. Record your observations and conclusion. In Electrical Energy, finish wiring, press Check my arrangement, test the switch and meters, then Record & check.

Automatic marks are practice checks. Your teacher’s assessment is separate.

Saving and moving devices

Sign in to a verified account to save online. Wait for Saved online before switching devices. Waiting to sync means a device recovery copy is retained; Save failed needs attention. My Learning lists saved work and offers exports and an explicit import of older device work.

Do not import somebody else’s work on a shared computer. Sign out when finished. If two devices edit the same work, both copies are retained until you choose one.

Classes and submissions

Enter the code supplied by your teacher in My Learning. After teacher approval, open saved work and submit a copy. Teachers see that copy and attached documents, not your private drafts. Read feedback in My Learning; revise your draft and submit another version if asked.

Storage and support

Private work has a 50 MB allowance per account; each document may be up to 10 MB. Export before removing work. Submitted attachments stay available while their submission is retained.
