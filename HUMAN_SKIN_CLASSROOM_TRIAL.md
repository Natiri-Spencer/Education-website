# Grade 7 Human Skin Lab Classroom Trial

**Review status:** Awaiting a science teacher's anatomy review and a supervised Grade 7 trial.

Open **Human Skin** in the local [Grade 7 Living Things and Environment section](http://127.0.0.1:8000/virtual-labs/grade/7/#living-things-environment-heading).

## Science teacher review

- Complete both challenges. Check that the epidermis is the thin outer layer, the dermis is thicker, and the fat lies beneath the skin.
- Check that the follicle encloses the hair root, that the shaft extends above the surface, and that the oil gland connects to the follicle.
- Check that the eccrine gland, duct, and pore form a continuous path and are visually distinct. Check that the vessels and sensory endings remain within the dermis.
- Review whether the prediction, hints, trace, and explanation questions make sense for Grade 7. Confirm that the cooling explanation refers to evaporation.

Reference illustrations and descriptions: [NCI Skin Anatomy](https://training.seer.cancer.gov/melanoma/anatomy/) and [OpenStax Accessory Structures of the Skin](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/5-2-accessory-structures-of-the-skin).

## Supervised learner trial

Ask learners to predict, assemble, check, revise, trace, explain, and save before trying the supporting structures. Observe at least one desktop session and one phone or tablet session if available. Note whether learners can use the placement controls without assistance, act on feedback after a misplaced part, explain which way sweat travels, and recover an unfinished model after a refresh. Check that their entry appears in **My lab notebooks**.

Record the reviewer and date, device types, what learners found difficult, and any corrections needed before release. Record observations without including learner names.
