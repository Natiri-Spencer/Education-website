A little room for big ideas

Write with a pen, then pause. The local handwriting recognizer suggests editable text and common maths notation. Review suggestions before applying them. Write separated printed letters; cursive writing may be unreliable. Auto handles both; choose Text & paragraphs for sentences, or Maths & numbers for isolated variables and integers. Leave a clear gap between words and a larger gap between paragraphs.

Use Select and double-click a block to fix a misreading. Enter adds a text line. The equation editor accepts (a)/(b), x^(2), a_(1), sqrt(x), integral(x^2,x), integral(x^2,x,0,1), diff(x^2,x), partial(f(x,y),x), and mathematical symbols. Voice equation understands general linear, quadratic, and cubic equations, powers, roots, fractions, decimals, inequalities, and common functions, then waits for your approval. The eraser removes handwriting and equations. Touch the board with four or more fingers to clear every element; Undo restores the cleared board. Graph plots functions such as y = x^2, or linear, quadratic and cubic graphs from an x–y table. Choose Table of values, enter your points, and add the graph; double-click it to edit the table later. Ask AI answers your typed question briefly. Answers appear in the dialog; use Add answer to whiteboard if you want to keep one on the board. Manual corrections stay as you edited them. Complex or unread notation can stay as ink. Undo restores your source strokes; Select also offers Restore original handwriting.
