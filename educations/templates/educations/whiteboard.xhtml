A little room for big ideas
Write with a pen, then pause. The local handwriting recognizer suggests editable text and common maths notation. Review suggestions before applying them. Write separated printed letters; cursive writing may be unreliable. Auto handles both; choose Text & paragraphs for sentences, or Maths & numbers for isolated variables and integers. Leave a clear gap between words and a larger gap between paragraphs.
Use Select and double-click a block to fix a misreading. Enter adds a text line. The equation editor accepts (a)/(b), x^(2), a_(1), sqrt(x), integral(x^2,x), integral(x^2,x,0,1), diff(x^2,x), partial(f(x,y),x), and mathematical symbols. Voice equation understands general linear, quadratic, and cubic equations, powers, roots, fractions, decimals, inequalities, and common functions, then waits for your approval. The eraser removes handwriting and equations. Use two fingers to move or zoom. More offers Clear annotations with confirmation, followed by Undo. Graph plots functions such as y = x^2, or linear, quadratic and cubic graphs from an x–y table. Choose Table of values, enter your points, and add the graph; double-click it to edit the table later. Manual corrections stay as you edited them. Complex or unread notation can stay as ink. Undo restores your source strokes; Select also offers Restore original handwriting.
