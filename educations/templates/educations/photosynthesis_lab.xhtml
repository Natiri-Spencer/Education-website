    About this model

    The plant has adequate water and carbon dioxide. The paper blocks light on both surfaces of the middle strip. Forty-eight hours in darkness and several hours in good light are represented in seconds. The four-hour exposure shown is illustrative; real results depend on conditions. Starch is evidence of photosynthesis, not a direct measurement of its rate.
