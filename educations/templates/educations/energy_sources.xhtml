PROCEDURE

  1. Bring the solar panel, bulb holder, bulb and wires from the shelf.
  2. Select one panel terminal, then one holder terminal to connect a wire. Connect the other two terminals.
  3. Observe the bulb in sunlight.
  4. Drag the book onto the panel. Change the cover from partial to full.
  5. Uncover the panel and compare. Record each observation.

Click apparatus or drag it onto the workbench. The bulb fits into its holder automatically when both are present.

About this model

Sunlight is converted to electrical energy by the panel. The bulb converts electrical energy to light and heat.

This is a qualitative model: uncovered means bright, partly covered means dim, and fully covered means off. Real partial shading depends on the panel's cell connections and can reduce output unevenly.
