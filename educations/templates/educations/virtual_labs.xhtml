PROCEDURE

  1. 1Place the mortar on the workbench.

  3. 3Crush the petals with the pestle, then return it to the shelf.
  4. 4Pour distilled water into the mortar. Observe the purple extract, then return the empty water beaker to the shelf.
  5. 5Place the empty beaker on the workbench.
  6. 6Filter the extract into the beaker, then return the funnel to the shelf.
  7. 7Select a sample solution to test.
  8. 8Add a few drops of indicator to the sample.
  9. 9Observe the colour and classify the solution.

What does the indicator show?

The virtual urine sample models standing urine made basic by ammonia released as bacteria break down urea. It turns the indicator green. Fresh urine pH varies. Sugar solution models sugar dissolved in pure water.

Colour guide for this simulation: red/pink suggests an acid, purple a neutral solution, and green a base.
