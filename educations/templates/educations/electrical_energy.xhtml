PROCEDURE

  1. Choose a connection arrangement.
  2. Bring the apparatus and connecting wires from the shelf.
  3. Select two terminals to connect a wire. You can also drag between terminals.
  4. Connect the ammeter in series. Connect the voltmeter across the source or bulb.
  5. Close the switch. Read the meters and observe the bulbs.
  6. Record your readings. Build another arrangement and compare.

Virtual dry cells only. Never connect a real ammeter directly across a cell or short a cell. Real parallel cells must be matched.

About this model

Guided assembly uses the positions and wiring of your chosen arrangement. Wires join only at terminals; a crossing does not make a junction.

Each ideal dry cell supplies 1.50 V. Identical bulbs have a fixed resistance of 10 Ω. Wires and the ammeter have zero resistance; the voltmeter draws no current. Brightness follows bulb power, relative to a bulb supplied with 3 V. Real bulbs have changing resistance, and real cells have internal resistance and limited capacity.

Matching cells in parallel supply the voltage of one cell. They can supply energy for longer; this simulation does not model discharge time. Moving dots show conventional current from positive to negative outside the cells, not the speed or direction of electrons.
