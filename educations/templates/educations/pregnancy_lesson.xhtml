Implantation and Pregnancy

Follow early cell division, attachment to the uterine lining, and development from embryo to baby.

Integrated Science 5-minute narrated lesson

What you will learn

  • How a blastocyst attaches to the uterine lining.
  • How the embryo develops into a fetus.
  • How the placenta, cord, and amniotic fluid support development.
  • What happens during the three trimesters and how gestation is counted.

Pause and think

Where does implantation happen? What does the placenta do? Why are 40 pregnancy weeks about 38 weeks after fertilisation?

Illustrations show simplified teaching views. Cells are enlarged, and months of development are compressed into minutes.
