Tutorial Procedure

  1. RULER
    • Drag the ruler onto the canvas, or click it to place it.
    • Choose Move equipment and drag the ruler body or its white handle.
    • Drag the blue end handle to rotate the ruler. You can also enter its angle in Construction Tools.
    • Double-click the ruler for vertical alignment.
  2. COMPASS
    • Choose Move equipment and drag the compass legs or white needle handle.
    • Choose Circle, then click the centre and a point on its edge.
    • The distance between the points sets the radius.
    • Drag the green rotation handle around the needle to draw an arc or circle.
    • Set an exact radius in Construction Tools.
  3. POINT
    • Select Point, then click to mark a position.
    • Points receive labels automatically.
  4. LINE
    • Bring the ruler onto the canvas.
    • Select Line. Click two points, or drag from the centre to the circle in any direction.
    • The line shows its length in centimetres.
  5. SECTOR
    • Select the sector activity.
    • Click a centre, then a point on its arc, then the end direction.
    • Read the arc length and perimeter, including both radii.
