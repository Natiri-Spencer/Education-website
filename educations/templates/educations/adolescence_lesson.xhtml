Physical Changes During Adolescence

Understand shared changes, typical changes in girls and boys, different timing, and simple personal care.

What you will learn

  • Changes shared by girls and boys, including growth, body hair, sweat, and skin changes.
  • Typical changes in girls and boys, including periods and voice changes.
  • Why puberty begins at different times.
  • Personal-care habits and how to ask for support.

Pause and think

Name two shared changes. Explain one typical change in girls and one in boys. Choose one helpful personal-care habit.

The figures are clothed teaching diagrams. Bodies vary, changes happen gradually, and nobody needs to share private body changes with classmates.
