Fertilisation: The Sperm's Journey

Follow sperm through the reproductive organs, watch one sperm join the egg, and learn what happens to the others.

Integrated Science 5-minute narrated lesson

What you will learn

  • The route from the vagina through the cervix and uterus to a fallopian tube.
  • How one sperm joins an egg to form a zygote.
  • Why other sperm stay outside and how unused sperm are cleared.

Pause and think

Where does fertilisation usually happen? How many sperm normally fertilise one egg?

Cells are enlarged and time is compressed to make the process easier to follow.
