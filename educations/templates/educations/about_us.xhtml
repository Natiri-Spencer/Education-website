ABOUT EDUPORTAL

Learn, teach, and experiment in one place.

EduPortal organizes CBC learning materials by grade and subject, and gives learners and teachers practical tools for active lessons. Explain ideas on the digital whiteboard, practise mathematics, and investigate science through guided virtual labs.

INTERACTIVE LEARNING

Teach and explore

WB

Digital teaching whiteboard

Write or draw freely, add images, move around an unlimited canvas, and save boards in the browser. Handwriting autocorrection proposes editable text and mathematics while preserving the original ink for review, undo, or restoration.

Open the whiteboard
LAB

Guided virtual labs

Explore interactive CBC science and mathematics activities with apparatus, ordered procedures, observations, calculations, and separate investigation notebooks. Grade 8 activities cover oxygen, osmosis, diffusion, elements and compounds, circles, and graph work.

Explore virtual labs

GETTING STARTED

Find what you need

  1. Choose a grade from the menu or the Home page.
  2. Select a subject to open its learning materials.
  3. Use the resource lists to browse PDFs, videos, and images.
  4. Open the Whiteboard or Virtual labs from the main menu for an interactive lesson.
