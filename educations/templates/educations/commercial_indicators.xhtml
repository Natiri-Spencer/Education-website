YOU ARE THE SCIENTIST

Plan your investigation

Which indicator gives you enough evidence to identify an acid, a base, or a neutral solution?

  1. Drag a clean beaker onto the bench.
  2. Choose a sample and pour it into your selected beaker.
  3. Predict a colour, then choose an indicator. For liquid indicators, bring a clean dropper, fill it from the chosen indicator bottle, then click or drag the filled dropper onto the sample.
  4. Observe, record, and compare another indicator on a fresh portion.
