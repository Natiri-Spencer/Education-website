# Original diagram design

The lesson's fully clothed learner figures, gradual growth comparisons, hormone-message pathway, skin model, voice model, timing bands and care icons are drawn directly by `tools/adolescence_visuals.cjs` using the Canvas 2D API.

The design uses the academy's navy, teal and gold palette, warm brown skin tones, loose everyday clothing, clear labels, and a separate learning-focus panel. It avoids ideal-body rankings and fixed age-to-body comparisons. The final frames are rendered at 1920 × 1080 and 24 fps.

These diagrams do not use newly generated raster portraits. The only reused raster asset is the female reproductive-organ cutaway for the menstruation explanation. Its original prompt is in `anatomy-visual-prompt.txt`.
