# UJI Pen Characters (Version 2)

Prat, F., Castro, M., Llorens, D., Marzal, A., & Vilar, J. (2008).
UJI Pen Characters (Version 2). UCI Machine Learning Repository.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.24432/C5FG8S
Source: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/177/uji+pen+characters+version+2
Download: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/static/public/177/uji+pen+characters+version+2.zip

https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The training program selects ASCII characters, normalizes/rasterizes the
recorded strokes, augments their geometry and joins characters into artificial
word samples. Training, validation and test writers remain disjoint. The
original dataset files retain their original content. The trained model is
an experimental derivative; its evaluation and limitations are recorded in
models/whiteboard/evaluation.json. No relationship or endorsement by the
dataset creators is implied.
